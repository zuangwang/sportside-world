Our approach

Protection isn’t a feature.
It’s the foundation.

01

Mentor review

Every mentor profile stays private until identity, experience, and references have been reviewed.

02

03

No open direct messages

Introductions follow a structured request flow instead of unrestricted private messaging.

04

Clear reporting

Athletes and families can flag concerns quickly. Reports are designed for prompt human review.
