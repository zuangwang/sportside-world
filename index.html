Built for trust

Safe support is
better support.

Sportside is designed to keep athletes, families, and mentors informed and protected at every step.

✓
Safety
first
Our approach

Protection isn’t a feature.
It’s the foundation.

01

Mentor review

Every mentor profile stays private until identity, experience, and references have been reviewed.

02

Guardian involvement

For younger athletes, guardians are included in introductions, permissions, and session planning.

03

No open direct messages

Introductions follow a structured request flow instead of unrestricted private messaging.

04

Clear reporting

Athletes and families can flag concerns quickly. Reports are designed for prompt human review.

!

Prototype notice
Real verification, moderation, consent records, and emergency workflows require a secure backend and trained operators before launch.
